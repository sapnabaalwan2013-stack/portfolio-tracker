Import from Chase

  1. Sign in at chase.com and open your Self-Directed Investing account.
  2. Go to Activity (or Statements & documents → Activity), choose a date range starting from your first trade, and click Download as CSV.
  3. Choose that file below. Columns are matched by name, so other brokers' CSVs work too.
